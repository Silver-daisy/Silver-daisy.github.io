Private Eden Academy

The Basics of Private Eden

This is a prestigious school with a high graduation rate steaming from the early 1800s as a college, It is open to a variety of Humanoid and Human like being who aren't magically gifted as magic within Private Eden is prohibited, many call these practices racists and discriminatory and the school stand strong in their beliefs and refuse to change them. Since they are strictly against monster, mythic, anthros and other such species a handful of the students disguise themselves to attend this academy. These halls have housed the most brightest mind over the years however with that brightest there has also been some darkness within the halls as over the years there have been rumors about missing persons along with each graduating class; However there has been no evidence to confirm any of the students have been missing nor have the families held the school or facility accountable for their disappearance.
